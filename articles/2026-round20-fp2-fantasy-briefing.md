## United States Grand Prix: the FP2 picture

Our post-FP2 simulation for round 20 is now live. These are **average Fantasy points across the simulations**, using the practice data available so far. Qualifying and the race have not happened; the numbers will change if new information arrives.

1. **Charles Leclerc** — 28.8 expected points at £24.1m. The middle 90% of simulated outcomes runs from -9.6 to 51.0 points.
2. **Kimi Antonelli** — 28.8 expected points at £27.1m. The middle 90% of simulated outcomes runs from -2.8 to 49.0 points.
3. **Max Verstappen** — 25.4 expected points at £28.2m. The middle 90% of simulated outcomes runs from -9.9 to 49.0 points.

The wide ranges show why a high average is only part of the decision: a strong result and a difficult weekend are both possible.
