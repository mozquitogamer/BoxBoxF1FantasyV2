## Brazilian Grand Prix: the FP2 picture

Our post-FP2 simulation for round 22 is now live. These are **average Fantasy points across the simulations**, using the practice data available so far. Qualifying and the race have not happened; the numbers will change if new information arrives.

The wide ranges show why a high average is only part of the decision: a strong result and a difficult weekend are both possible.
