## Qatar Grand Prix: the FP2 picture

Our post-FP2 simulation for round 24 is now live. These are **average Fantasy points across the simulations**, using the practice data available so far. Qualifying and the race have not happened; the numbers will change if new information arrives.

## Leading drivers

1. **Max Verstappen** — 39.0 expected points at £29.4m. The middle 90% of simulated outcomes runs from -2.5 to 61.4 points.
2. **Kimi Antonelli** — 30.9 expected points at £27.7m. The middle 90% of simulated outcomes runs from -4.6 to 52.9 points.
3. **Lando Norris** — 26.0 expected points at £26.3m. The middle 90% of simulated outcomes runs from -9.9 to 48.6 points.

The wide ranges show why a high average is only part of the decision: a strong result and a difficult weekend are both possible.

**Carlos Sainz** has the highest expected points among drivers priced at or below the field's median price: 5.3 points at £8.4m. That makes them a useful budget comparison, although the projection alone does not account for the rest of your team's balance.
