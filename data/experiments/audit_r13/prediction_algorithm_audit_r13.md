# Prediction Algorithm Audit — Through 2026 Hungarian GP (Internal R13)

Date: 2026-07-27

## Executive decision

The production position models were rebuilt and retrained on all data available
through the Hungarian GP. The selected configuration is:

- XGBoost LambdaMART position rankers.
- 2026 event weight: **4.0x** (previously 2.5x).
- Qualifying: 1,200 trees.
- Post-qualifying race: 650 trees, trained with actual qualifying position.
- Actionable post-FP race: 650 trees, trained with event-prequential predicted
  qualifying position.
- Sprint: 400 trees; the inferior sprint-FP candidate is not saved.
- All 14 restored FP telemetry features are active.
- Unversioned manual driver/team ratings are excluded from every position
  ranker.
- Observed session weather is excluded from every position ranker until a
  timestamped lock-time forecast archive exists.
- The FP qualifying blend remains enabled, including the hard-track ramp.
- Race grid anchoring is disabled (`GRID_ANCHOR_CEIL = 0.0`) pending more
  hard-track evidence.
- Weather still affects wet-event training emphasis and the Monte Carlo model;
  manual wet/cold ratings remain available there as conditional adjustments.

The exact deployed tree counts achieved a strict 2026 event-prequential
recency-weighted composite MAE of **2.016** across the 11 contested rounds.

## Evaluation contract

Model selection uses `pipeline/evaluate_prequential_2026.py`. For every target
event:

```text
train = season before target season
     OR same season and round strictly before target round
test  = the one target event
```

The actionable race model never receives actual qualifying as a fallback.
Historical and target-event qualifying inputs are generated by models that
could only see earlier events.

Primary selection metric:

```text
0.30 * qualifying recency-weighted MAE
+ 0.25 * post-qualifying race recency-weighted MAE
+ 0.45 * actionable post-FP race recency-weighted MAE
```

The race-FP target has the largest weight because it is the prediction available
before the F1 Fantasy lock. Recency decay is 0.90 per round. Driver rows from
the same race are not treated as independent observations; comparisons are
paired at race-event level.

DNFs/DNS/DSQs are excluded from the finish-order ranker evaluation. Retirement
risk and severity are evaluated separately in the fantasy/Monte Carlo layer.

The controlled screens below use 250 trees for speed and identical folds.

| Configuration | Quali RW MAE | Race RW MAE | Race-FP RW MAE | Composite |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Old 2.5x weight + unversioned ratings | 2.068 | **1.849** | 2.219 | 2.081 |
| 4.0x + no unversioned ratings | **2.020** | 1.921 | **2.106** | **2.034** |

The selected integrity-safe configuration improves the controlled composite by

1. pre-qualifying accuracy is the product deadline;
2. the rating baseline is retrospectively contaminated; and
3. this year's data is explicitly the priority.

## Weight sweep

Strict 2026 composite at 250 trees:

| 2026 weight | Composite MAE |
|---:|---:|
| 1.0x | 2.073 |
| 1.5x | 2.121 |
| 2.5x | 2.081 |

Four times was the best tested value. Versus 2.5x it improved 8 events, tied

## Feature and algorithm experiments

### Restored FP telemetry

Dropping the 14 restored FP fields increased the composite from 2.016 to 2.111
in the controlled 4x screen. It regressed:

- qualifying by about 0.069 mean MAE;
- post-quali race by about 0.059; and
- actionable race-FP by about 0.122.

All 14 remain active.

### Manual ratings

The current ratings table was authored after 2026 R2 and has no effective-date
snapshots. Applying those values to 2020-early 2026 lets later judgments affect
earlier folds. The fields also accounted for roughly 8-10% of stale artifact
importance.

These are now excluded from the position rankers:

`tire_mgmt`, `wet_skill`, `overtaking`, `quali_skill`,
`quali_skill_x_ot_diff`, `strategy_rating`, `adaptability`, `cold_skill`, and
`strategy_sc_advantage`.

The columns remain in the data for diagnostics and conditional Monte Carlo
weather adjustments. Reintroduction requires effective-dated snapshots.

### XGBoost versus CatBoost

On the selected 4x/no-rating configuration:

- CatBoost improved retrospective post-quali race mean MAE by about 0.070.
- CatBoost worsened actionable race-FP mean MAE by about 0.077.
- The race-paired result was not decisive.

XGBoost remains selected because it was better on the deadline-relevant target
and avoids adding a second production inference path without a robust win.

### Tree counts

The exact 1,200/650 production counts improved the total score over the
250-tree screen. For race-FP, a 400-tree follow-up differed from 650 trees on
only two of 11 events: each won one event, with nine exact ties. It improved
recency-weighted MAE by only 0.004 while worsening unweighted mean MAE by 0.005.
The 650-tree setting is retained to avoid selecting on noise.

### Historical guardrail

Removing current manual ratings produced a mild 2023-2025 retrospective
trade-off:

- qualifying overall guardrail MAE: approximately +0.064;
- race overall guardrail MAE: approximately +0.012.

This does not justify using a known outcome-informed table. The 2026
pre-lock improvement and correct temporal semantics take precedence.

## Track and overlay audit

### Track similarity

The nine-dimensional circuit vectors are too homogeneous:

- median pairwise cosine similarity is about 0.975;
- 71.4% of track pairs are at least 0.95 similar;
- similarity-weighted rolling form is almost identical to normal rolling form
  (Spearman approximately 0.999).

Removing the similarity features slightly worsened the selected screen, so
they remain for now. They should be redesigned rather than silently removed:
standardize dimensions, validate each dimension independently, and reduce
duplicated form fields.

### FP qualifying blend

The exact Stage-B deployment evaluator tested raw model output, the normal
blend, the hard-track ramp, and grid anchoring.

| Policy | Race MAE |
|---|---:|
| No FP blend, no anchor | 3.235 |
| Current hard-track FP ramp, no anchor | **3.168** |
| Current hard-track FP ramp + anchor | 3.143 |

The hard FP ramp improved both completed hard-track cases and is retained.

### Grid anchoring

The aggregate anchor result looks slightly better, but the completed
Monaco/Hungary evidence is contradictory:

- Monaco: adding the anchor worsened race MAE by 0.20.
- Hungary: adding the anchor improved race MAE by about 0.47.

With only two directly relevant completed hard-track cases, the effect is not
stable enough for a large deterministic weight (up to 0.85). Anchoring is
disabled while the mechanism and tests remain available for future evaluation.

### Reused overtaking difficulty

`overtaking_difficulty` affects multiple layers: FP blend scaling, grid anchor,
overtake damping, and some Monte Carlo behavior. That can unintentionally
compound a single subjective rating. Disabling the anchor removes the largest
current double-count. Longer term, use separately calibrated variables for:

- qualifying importance;
- race passing resistance;
- incident/variance effects; and
- overtake-count expectation.

### Other track/manual inputs

- `pit_stop_speed` in the track classification is currently unused.
- `wet_skill`/`cold_skill` were formerly available both to the ranker and MC,
  creating double-counting; they are now MC-only.
- DOTD overrides, DNF-cause labels, official overtake counts, pit-stop points,
  and fantasy prices affect fantasy rankings but not the learned position
  order directly.

The detailed transform inventory is in
`data/experiments/audit_r13/manual_ratings_track_transform_audit.md`.

## Leakage and correctness fixes made

1. Constructor, team, and field aggregates are now shifted at event level
   before being joined back to drivers.
2. Global future-aware median imputation was removed; missing priors remain
   `NaN` for XGBoost.
3. Season progress now uses active race ordinal rather than cancelled calendar
   numbering.
4. Training cutoffs are applied before the 2026 weight ramp, and same-event
   exclusions are no longer called temporally safe.
5. Live prediction stubs preserve training-time `NaN` semantics.
6. Race training and validation share the same classified-finisher mask.
7. Sprint outcome targets and future main-qualifying features were removed
   from sprint inputs.
8. CatBoost receives proper event/query weights.
9. Session-matched wet weights are used in final and walk-forward training.
10. Unknown FP schema columns now fail the build instead of disappearing
    silently.
11. Partial-sector theoretical-best calculations were corrected.
12. Wrong-round forecast files are rejected rather than reused.
13. Single-round weather refreshes preserve other rounds and retain the old
    session row if a reload fails.
14. Post-race `Lapped`/`+N Laps` classifications are no longer treated as DNFs.
15. True DNF/DNS/DSQ rows retain a classified position separately while
    `race_position` remains null.
16. The weekend orchestrator now uses the correct all-history rebuild and
    preserves canonical OpenF1 overtake data.

## Hungarian GP post-race status

The internal R13 Hungarian post-race pipeline completed. Driver of the Day and
the three retirement causes were added from official/team reporting, and the
actual classification was rebuilt with the corrected semantics.

Official driver and constructor fantasy totals, official overtakes, official
pit-stop points, and post-R13 prices have now been recorded. R13 reconciles

absolute difference. The full recorded season is now exact for 242/242 driver

Stationary pit-stop telemetry is still unavailable, but it is no longer a
scoring blocker because the official constructor pit-stop points are used as
ground truth. R13 is now eligible for phase-specific calibration and has been
included in the preliminary post-quali calibration.

## Recommendation implementation status

1. **Lock-time weather archives — implemented.** Every successful Open-Meteo
   retrieval now writes immutable raw and processed snapshots with retrieval
   time, target session, provider, and raw-response hash. The mutable website
   forecast records the selected snapshot ID.
2. **Effective-dated manual ratings — implemented conservatively.** The first
   known rating table is versioned as `post_2026_r02`; all earlier events
   receive neutral values rather than present-day hindsight. No historical
   rating snapshots were invented. Manual ratings remain excluded from the
   position rankers.
3. **Immutable phase archives — implemented.** `pre_fp`, `post_fp`, and
   `post_quali` archives freeze on first write. `--force` cannot replace them;
   corrections require the explicit `--replace-phase-archive` option and leave
   a separate audit trail.
4. **Phase-safe confidence calibration — implemented.** Calibration reads only
   frozen phase archives with official actual totals, excludes reconstructed
   files by default, uses the correct 1.645 conversion for central P5-P95
   intervals, and stores cumulative snapshots. Inference may load only a
   snapshot ending before its target round.
5. **Track-vector diagnostics and evidence gates — implemented.** The current
   vectors are flagged as cosine-saturated (71.4% of circuit pairs have
   similarity at least 0.95). The diagnostic is persisted for redesign work,
   while hard-track grid anchoring remains disabled until at least four
   validated eligible events exist.
6. **R14 prospective holdout — started and frozen.** The genuine pre-FP Dutch
   GP archive is registered with SHA-256
   `bc3d4205db6d3515eb10cbe9a81948b056db2c7500a4f574940027b8dbf87def`.
   Its manifest records the exact qualifying/race model identities and no
   available weather snapshot. The post-FP archive will freeze separately
   after the relevant sessions.

Phase-specific calibration currently has:

- `post_fp`: 7 official, non-reconstructed rounds (154 driver rows), 90%
  interval coverage 78.6%, multiplier 1.3237;
- `pre_fp`: 6 official, non-reconstructed rounds (132 driver rows), 90%
  interval coverage 81.1%, multiplier 1.2520; and
- `post_quali`: 2 official, non-reconstructed rounds (44 driver rows), 90%
  interval coverage 72.7%. The preliminary multiplier is conservatively
  capped at 1.10 until at least three rounds are available.
